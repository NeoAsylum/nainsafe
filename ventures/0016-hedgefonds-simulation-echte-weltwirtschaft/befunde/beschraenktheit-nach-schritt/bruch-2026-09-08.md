## Finding 5 — Verdacht: the clamp sits behind an addition that can abort

`schritt.cpp:687` — `festkomma::plus(schreiber.lies_alt(platz), wirkung)` runs **before**
`min/max`. `spiel.md`'s `min(10'000, max(0, alt + …))` is total over integers; over `i64`
the inner sum is not. `probe_zustimmung_klemmt_statt_vortrag` deliberately starts two
countries at `I64_MAX` and `I64_MIN` and asserts they land on their bound — it passes
**only because `wirkung == 0`**. The day `zustimmung_elastizitaet ≠ 0` *and* one
instrument stand moves, that same probe flips to `"plus: Summe ausserhalb von i64 (T7)"`
without anyone touching `schritt.cpp` or the probe.

**Marked Verdacht because it is not reproducible today:** `wirkung ≠ 0` needs both
`schritt_3_politik` to compute and the placeholder in `parameter.toml:746` to be filled.
Neither exists. Proposal `0240`.
